# Project Brief

*Owner: Doan · Ticket: YC-6 · Last updated: 2026-09-25*

## User and decision

**Primary user:** An early-stage founder (or founder-to-be) who is building,
or considering building, a product in a specific solution space.

**Decision:** Whether their solution space is getting crowded among recent YC
batches, and therefore whether to proceed, differentiate, or reposition.

**How they use it:** The founder describes their space, reviews comparable
YC-funded companies, and sees how the number and share of companies in that
space have changed across batches, with a focus on recent ones.

**Secondary user:** An analyst choosing which 2–3 themes deserve a deeper
market study next quarter. Same data and metric, viewed as a ranking across
all themes rather than a drill-down into one. Deferred to a later sprint if
time is short.

**Open questions (to resolve after data profiling, Sprint 1–2):**
- How does a founder define their space: tags, industry labels, keyword
  search on company descriptions, or a combination?
- What counts as "crowded"? Crowding should be judged at the level of
  solution × application (what is built, and for which problem or
  customer), not technology alone. Candidate measures: company count,
  share of batch, growth relative to YC overall, and share still active,
  all within a given solution-application pair.
- What window counts as "recent batches," given that the newest companies may
  be under-counted in a directory of launched companies?
- Can competitor performance be measured? The source feed has no performance
  data (revenue, users, growth). Candidate sources are being evaluated in YC-8;
  if none is usable, maturity signals (team size, status, stage, hiring) will
  be used as clearly labelled proxies.
